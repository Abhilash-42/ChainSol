# chainSol: Supply Chain Risk Analyst

**chainSol** is an intelligent, AI-driven reasoning agent built with Python. Developed for the **Microsoft Agents League Hackathon (Reasoning Track)**, it maps macro-level global market and geopolitical disruptions down to micro-level enterprise product impacts in real-time, delivering automated risk mitigation paths.

---

## Problem Statement
In today’s volatile global market, geopolitical conflicts, trade shocks, and sudden maritime port closures create massive waves of disruption across global supply chains. When a critical shipping hub shuts down, enterprise leaders struggle to quickly answer a fundamental question: 
*"Which of our specific product lines, manufacturing hubs, and business units will this affect, and how do we pivot immediately?"*

---

## Architectural Workflow & Microsoft IQ Integration
chainSol simulates a highly sophisticated multi-step reasoning workflow by integrating key concepts from Microsoft's intelligence layer requirements:

1. **Disruption Monitoring:** The system monitors global events (e.g., severe constraints or shutdowns at major international ports).
2. **Semantic Graph Mapping (Simulating Fabric IQ):** Maps the macro location data directly to internal enterprise data graphs—instantly linking the affected port to specific suppliers, sub-assemblies, and part numbers.
3. **Blast Radius Analysis (Simulating Microsoft Foundry):** Traces dependencies up to the finished product lines, evaluating inventory runways, calculating revenue exposure, and displaying a multi-step logic thought process.
4. **Automated Strategic Mitigation:** Generates immediate contingency directives, such as drafting automated RFQs for pre-vetted backup suppliers and highlighting alternative maritime routing.
